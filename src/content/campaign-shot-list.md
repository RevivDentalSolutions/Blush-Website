# Blush editorial campaign shot list

This is the production and approval sheet for the six original service portraits. The attached client reference is an **art-direction reference only**, never an image source or face reference.

| Asset | Unmistakable focal point | Model distinction | Required crop | Reject when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Powder Brows | Both complete softly graduated brows and individual hairs | Golden medium skin; hazel-green eyes; dark hair fully swept back | Collarbone-up, forehead included | Hair, shadow, hands, or crop hides either brow |
| Lip Blushing | Symmetrical rosy-nude lips, natural border, realistic satin texture | Deep golden skin; gray-green eyes; smooth hair away from face | Shoulders-up, lips close to center | Lip color is red, opaque, overlined, or visually secondary |
| Permanent Eyeliner | Both pale eyes and refined upper lash lines; brows remain visible | Warm bronze skin; pale green-gray eyes; brunette hair pulled back | Close face crop retaining full forehead and chin | Wing is dramatic, lashes obscure liner, or either eye is cropped |
| ProCell Microchanneling | Luminous real skin with pores, fine lines, and dimensional highlights | Mature adult, honey-bronze skin, minimal makeup | Full face, collarbone-up | Retouching looks plastic or makeup becomes the subject |
| Scar Camouflage | Tasteful fully healed skin and subtle tonal variation | Deep skin tone; non-identifying body detail | Abstract close crop with clear skin context | Image resembles a wound, procedure, or promised result |
| Stretch Mark Revision | Natural mature stretch marks and real skin texture | Medium-deep bronze skin; non-identifying body detail | Abstract close crop, dignified composition | Texture is erased or image implies complete removal |

## Campaign-wide production controls

- Adult models only; no celebrity or identifiable-person resemblance request.
- Vertical master: 1600×2200. Horizontal alternate: 2400×1600. Keep the treatment feature inside the central mobile-safe 60% of both.
- Warm cocoa, taupe, champagne, bronze, or mocha seamless studio background only.
- One sculpted warm key, restrained fill, gentle eye catchlights, realistic highlight rolloff, and shallow studio depth.
- Retain pores, brow hairs, lip lines, fine lines, and natural facial asymmetry. Retouch temporary blemishes conservatively; never reshape anatomy.
- No products, palettes, brushes, treatment rooms, equipment, outdoor scenes, eyewear, hands over the face, bangs over brows, bright red lips, heavy editorial makeup, embedded text, logos, or watermarks.
- Editorial assets must remain labeled as brand imagery and must never enter the real-client result gallery.

## Approval gate

Review each vertical and horizontal image at desktop card, mobile card, desktop hero, and mobile hero crops. Hide all surrounding copy and ask: **“Could someone identify the service from the photo without reading the title?”** Reject and regenerate any image that does not pass immediately.
